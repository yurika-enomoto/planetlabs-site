About PlanetDAO

Planet Labs provides operational support for PlanetDAO initiatives. While PlanetDAO manages investment and shared ownership, Planet Labs oversees the development and management of cultural and historical assets structured as independent limited companies. Through partnerships with local operators, we facilitate infrastructure development, accommodation operations and community coordination.

Supported by PlanetDAO projects

In addition to managing PlanetDAO-owned projects, Planet Labs also supports selected external initiatives under the “Supported by PlanetDAO” label. These projects are not owned or operated by PlanetDAO but are aligned with its mission. Support may include sales facilitation, promotion, or strategic collaboration. As these projects follow a different structure, they are not represented in the diagram above.
